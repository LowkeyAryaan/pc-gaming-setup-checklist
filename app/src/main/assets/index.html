Terms & Conditions.

Please review the terms before using your setup planner.

1. Purpose

This is a personal PC and gaming setup planning tool. It helps you track products, estimated costs, contributions and savings. It is not a seller or financial service.

2. Pricing and purchases

Product prices, availability, seller information and external links may change. Verify compatibility, retailer legitimacy, warranties and final prices before purchasing anything. Purchases happen outside the app.

3. Your device and data

Your presets, product details, images, preferences and savings records are stored on your device. The app does not provide an online account or automatic cloud backup. Clearing app storage or uninstalling may permanently erase this information. Export backups regularly.

4. Currency settings

Changing a currency only changes its label and formatting. Existing amounts are never converted automatically. Enter correct values yourself for the currency you select.

5. User responsibility

You are responsible for the details you enter and the purchases you choose to make. This app is provided as a planning aid without a guarantee that product links, prices or calculations meet your personal circumstances.

6. Feedback by email

Feedback comments are saved locally until you choose to email one. Sending feedback opens your email app with the recipient aryaanmikaeel@gmail.com and your message filled in. Nothing is automatically delivered. Your email provider may disclose your sender address to the recipient when you press Send.
